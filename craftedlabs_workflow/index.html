Trust and fulfilment

Payments & secure delivery

Payment is not complete because a browser displays a success message. Your backend must verify the provider's notification before unlocking a file.

Security critical

Purchase-to-download workflow

01Choose product

Customer reads details and selects Buy.

02Create pending order

Server loads the trusted price and creates a unique order reference.

03Hosted checkout

Server creates a payment session with the provider.

04Verify webhook

Check signature, transaction, order, amount and currency server-side.

05Grant entitlement

Mark paid and record exactly which product the customer owns.

06Deliver securely

Issue a short-lived signed URL after checking ownership.

Security rules — do not compromise

Provider options to investigate

Lemon SqueezyDigital sales / Merchant of Record
GumroadHosted digital-product selling
FlutterwavePayment gateway; local methods

Confirm Rwanda seller eligibility, account age, identity checks, payout currency, fees, refund handling and business requirements directly with each provider before committing.

Never: unlock downloads from a success-page URL, expose a public PDF link, trust a price sent by the browser, or put private payment credentials in JavaScript.
Correct principle: Provider-confirmed payment → verified backend event → paid order → customer entitlement → time-limited download link.

Pre-launch checks

0% done

Before accepting payments

Check your legal ability to operate the seller account. If you are under the provider's required age, a parent or legal guardian must handle the account in line with its terms. Never use false identity details.

Also check local business, tax, consumer-protection and privacy obligations. This workflow is a planning guide, not legal or tax advice.

Launch rule

Do not launch paid downloads until you have completed a real end-to-end test: payment, webhook verification, order status, file permission, email and refund path.
